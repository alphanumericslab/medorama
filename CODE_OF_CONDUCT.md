# Code of conduct

Medorama adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other space of the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and level of
experience, give and accept criticism of work rather than of people, and never harass anyone.

**Patient data are part of the conduct.** Never post patient data, real recordings, or
screenshots of real data in an issue, a pull request or a discussion, even when they seem
harmless; use the synthetic data of `tools/make-demo-data.py` instead ([PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)).

**Reporting.** Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, Reza Sameni (through
the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile). Reports are handled confidentially, and the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant apply.
