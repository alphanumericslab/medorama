      At most 64 waveform lanes are shown at once; tick and untick them under Waveforms. A signal recorded twice at the same time has a second lane (II (2)). Wheel over the lane labels scrolls the lanes. Values are in the units stated in the file; adu means the file states no calibration. Shaded grey: break in the data. Amber: time not stated in the file (placed next to its neighbours). With the qc_verbose setting, a dashed blue outline marks data stored out of order that the reader put back in order (not a break).
