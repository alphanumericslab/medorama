Medorama is free to use, study, change and share for noncommercial purposes under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Any other use needs a separate commercial
license from the author. This page explains the difference and how to obtain a commercial
license. The license text itself is authoritative; this page summarizes it.

## Use that needs no further permission

The license permits use for any **noncommercial purpose**, and names two cases explicitly:

- **Personal use** for research, experiment and testing for the benefit of public knowledge,
  personal study, hobby projects and similar purposes, without any anticipated commercial
  application.
- **Use by noncommercial organizations**: charitable organizations, educational institutions,
  public research organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental
  protection organizations and government institutions, regardless of how that use is funded.

Within those purposes you may run Medorama, change it, build on it and distribute it, provided
that everyone who receives a copy also receives the license terms (or their URL) and the
`Required Notice:` line at the top of [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Use that needs a commercial license

Use for a commercial purpose needs a commercial license. Examples:

- a company using Medorama in its own operations, research or product development;
- offering Medorama, or a service or product built on it, to customers;
- consulting, hosting or integration work for which Medorama is used on behalf of a paying
  client, when the use itself is for that client's commercial purpose;
- including Medorama, or parts of it, in a commercial product.

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, ask before you use it.

## How to obtain a commercial license

Contact the author, Reza Sameni (<https://sameni.info>), with a short description of your
organization and the intended use. Commercial licenses are agreed case by case, in writing.

## Components under other licenses

Medorama's programs include third-party components, each under its own license, listed in
[THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md). A commercial license for Medorama covers
Medorama's own code; the terms of those components continue to apply to them. In particular,
WFDB records are read with [wfdb-x](https://github.com/alphanumericslab/wfdb-x), a separate
project under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later. Commercial licensees may use a
build without WFDB support, which has no copyleft dependency, or one with it, whose wfdb-x
component stays under its own license ([docs/building.md](docs/building.md#without-wfdb)).
